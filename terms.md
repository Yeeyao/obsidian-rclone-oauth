# Terms of Service

Last updated: November 5, 2026

## 1. Purpose

Obsidian-Rclone is a personal, non-commercial OAuth
configuration for synchronizing Obsidian notes between
a local computer and Google Drive using rclone.

## 2. Personal Use

The configuration is intended only for its owner.

No public synchronization service, user registration,
or paid service is offered.

## 3. Data Management

Synchronization is initiated and managed by the owner.

Synchronization operations may modify or delete files
on the local computer or in Google Drive.

The owner is responsible for maintaining independent
backups of important files.

## 4. Third-Party Software

This configuration uses rclone and Google Drive.

These services and applications are governed by their
respective terms and policies.

## 5. Discontinuation

The owner may stop using this configuration at any time
and revoke its Google OAuth authorization.
